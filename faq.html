Booking & payment

Send the route, date and number of passengers through the booking form, WhatsApp, the Trip Desk chat on this site or a phone call. You get a written all-inclusive quote back, usually within fifteen minutes. A twenty percent advance confirms the vehicle.

Fuel, tolls, parking, state entry taxes, driver allowance and, on multi-day trips, the driver's food and accommodation. Protected-area permit fees, monument tickets and the Bhutan Sustainable Development Fee are charged at actuals because those rates are set by the authorities, not by us.

Twenty percent advance by UPI or bank transfer holds the car. The balance is handed to the driver in cash or paid by UPI at the end of the trip. Corporate accounts are billed monthly against a GST invoice.

On the road

Give us the flight number when you book. The driver tracks it and waits. The first sixty minutes after landing carry no waiting charge, and for genuine airline delays we do not charge waiting at all.

Vehicles & safety

Below about 2,000 m a sedan is comfortable and cheaper. For Lachen, Lachung, Gurudongmar, Zuluk and Nathu La you want a Scorpio or Bolero for the ground clearance. Groups of five or more travelling long circuits are happiest in an Innova Crysta. Tell us the route and we will recommend honestly, including when the cheaper car is fine.

Every vehicle carries a valid commercial permit, comprehensive insurance, fitness certificate and pollution certificate. Documents are in the car and can be shown on request.

Police verification, a valid commercial licence with badge, a road test on a gradient of our choosing, and two supervised runs on any new route before carrying guests. High-altitude routes are rostered only to drivers with three or more seasons.

Yes. Cars going above 3,500 m carry a portable oxygen can and a first-aid box. Gurudongmar sits at 5,430 m, so we also advise a rest day in Lachen first.

Cancellations

Cancelling more than 48 hours before pickup is free and the advance is refunded in full within seven working days. Inside 48 hours the advance is held as credit against another trip within six months.

If we cannot provide the vehicle we booked, we arrange an equivalent or better one at no extra cost. If no vehicle can be provided at all, the entire amount paid is refunded.

If the army closes Nathu La or snow shuts a North Sikkim road, permit fees already paid to the authorities are not refundable to us and so cannot be refunded to you. Our own service charge for that day is waived and we offer an alternative sightseeing route instead.

Car rental, airport transfers and tour packages across Sikkim, Darjeeling, Bhutan and the North East since 2012. Own fleet, own drivers, fixed fares.
