Here is how Saha Tours and Travels works and who drives for us.

Our story

Built by people who drive these roads themselves

Saha Tours and Travels began in 2012 in Siliguri, with a small fleet and a simple goal — to provide reliable transportation for every journey. What started locally grew through word of mouth, taking us across the Sikkim and Darjeeling corridors and beyond.

What has changed since then is the size of our network. What has stayed the same is our approach to every journey — clear communication, reliable service and pricing you can understand from the start.

Today, we have 30+ vehicles and 40+ drivers, covering 20+ destinations across Sikkim, Darjeeling, Bhutan and the wider Northeast. Our team works to ensure smooth transportation and timely support, whether it's an airport transfer, a local journey or a multi-day trip.

The drivers

Nobody drives for us on day one

A new driver rides the route twice with a senior before he takes a guest. On North Sikkim and Bhutan runs, only drivers with three seasons or more are rostered.

Police verification

Character certificate and address verification on file before the first duty.

Hill endorsement

Commercial licence with a valid badge, plus a road test on a gradient we choose.

Route familiarity

Two supervised runs on any new corridor before he carries paying guests on it.

Guest feedback

Every trip is rated. Two poor ratings and the driver goes back to supervised duty.
