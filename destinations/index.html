Destinations we drive

Sikkim, the Darjeeling hills, Bhutan and the wider North East. What each one is like, what needs a permit, and when to actually go.

27.33°N · 1,650 m

Sikkim

Gangtok, Pelling, Lachung, Yumthang, Zuluk, Nathu La

Best season: October to May
Destination

Sikkim

Sikkim is the reason most people come here. Gangtok sits at a comfortable 1,650 m and works as the base, but the trip people remember is the run north to Lachung and the Yumthang valley, or east on the old Silk Route to Zuluk's thirty-two hairpin bends.

Everything above Chungthang sits inside a protected area, so permits are compulsory and only registered Sikkim-plated vehicles are allowed. We hold that registration, which is why our cars can take you the whole way instead of dropping you at the checkpost.

What people go for

Tsomgo Lake & Baba MandirNathu La pass (Wed–Sun)Yumthang Valley & Zero PointGurudongmar LakePelling SkywalkRavangla Buddha ParkZuluk & Nathang ValleyRumtek Monastery

October to May. June to September is monsoon and North Sikkim roads close often.

Darjeeling

Darjeeling, Kalimpong, Mirik, Kurseong, Lepchajagat

Best season: All year
Destination

Darjeeling

Three hours from the plains and a different climate entirely. Darjeeling is compact enough to see in two days, which leaves room for Kalimpong's monasteries and nurseries or a quiet night at Lepchajagat away from the crowds.

The Tiger Hill sunrise run leaves at 3:30am and the queue for the viewing tower forms early. Our drivers know which side road avoids the jam, and in peak season we book the vehicle slot the previous evening.

What people go for

Tiger Hill sunriseBatasia Loop & war memorialDarjeeling Himalayan Railway toy trainHappy Valley tea estatePadmaja Naidu Himalayan ZooMirik LakeDeolo Hill, KalimpongLepchajagat forest stay

All year. October to December gives the clearest Kanchenjunga views.

Bhutan

Phuentsholing, Thimphu, Punakha, Paro, Dochula

Best season: March to June and September to November
Destination

Bhutan

The land border at Phuentsholing is a four-hour drive from Bagdogra, and for Indian nationals entry is by permit rather than visa. Thimphu, Punakha and Paro form the standard circuit, with the Tiger's Nest hike as the day everyone plans around.

Permits are filed at the Phuentsholing immigration office and normally take one working day, which is why our itineraries put the first night at the border. Route permits for Punakha and beyond are issued separately in Thimphu. We file both.

What people go for

Tiger's Nest (Taktsang)Punakha DzongDochula pass & 108 chortensBuddha Dordenma, ThimphuTashichho DzongChele La passParo Rinpung DzongThimphu weekend market

March to June and September to November. Book six weeks ahead for the spring festivals.

North East

Shillong, Cherrapunji, Dawki, Kaziranga, Guwahati, Tawang

Best season: September to April
Destination

North East

Meghalaya and Assam are a different kind of trip — longer driving days, lower altitude, far greener. Cherrapunji's waterfalls, the living root bridges at Nongriat and the glass-clear Umngot at Dawki are the highlights, with Kaziranga's rhinos a day's drive east.

Distances here look small on a map and take twice as long in practice. We roster two drivers on the Guwahati–Tawang run and pre-book stays, because turning up without a reservation in Dirang in season does not end well.

What people go for

Double-decker root bridge, NongriatNohkalikai FallsUmngot river, DawkiMawlynnong villageKaziranga safarisKamakhya TempleSela Pass & TawangMajuli island

September to April. Meghalaya is spectacular in the monsoon but the treks turn dangerous.
